Privacy

Entries on this site have Like and Dislike buttons. Pressing one keeps a random id in your browser and your vote in a Google Firebase database. Your vote and the random id can be read by anyone.

No name, email address or account is involved. Google sees your internet address when you press a button or read the counts. Google's own privacy policy says what Google does with this information: policies.google.com/privacy.
